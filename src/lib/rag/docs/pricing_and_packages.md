# Whizzly Lab — Pricing, Packages & Financial Framework

## Transparent Pricing Philosophy
Whizzly Lab provides clear, honest, milestone-based engineering pricing strictly in **USD**.
- **No Hidden Fees**: Transparent rate benchmarks with zero surprise retainer billing.
- **100% IP Ownership**: You own all source code, trained neural model weights, schemas, and configurations from day one.
- **Milestone Structure**: Typical engagement structure is 50% upfront to initiate the architecture sprint, and 50% upon final staging delivery and sign-off.
- **Sprint Delivery**: Rapid working increments from 2-week MVPs to multi-month enterprise pod deployments.
